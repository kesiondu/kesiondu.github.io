In Mottle, the move that keeps you alive is often letting go. You steer a chameleon across patches of color, collect glowing Fireflies, and stop long enough to blend into the ground before a Seeker's beam sweeps past.

**Mottle: Hide & Seek** is a free browser game created by Star Lord and originally published on [8x.gg](https://www.8x.gg/apps/xPKXL5uurAzb6pAG). [Mottle Game](https://mottlegame.cc/) is our independent companion site. It brings the original player, controls, and beginner tips together so you can learn the basics and start a round from the same page.

## Controls: drag to move, release to blend

| Action | Mouse | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move the chameleon | Hold the mouse button and drag inside the game area | Drag one finger inside the game area |
| Stop and begin blending | Release the mouse button | Lift your finger |
| Pause or mute | Use the on-screen controls | Tap the on-screen controls |

Camouflage takes time to build after you stop. Pick a stopping point before you move, and leave enough time to blend before the next sweep.

## Play your first round in five steps

1. **Use the Hide countdown to plan one short route.** Find your chameleon, a nearby Firefly, and a patch where you can stop after collecting it.
2. **Watch the nearest Seeker.** Follow the edge of its light cone and wait for it to move away from your route. A Firefly that looks close can still be a poor target if the beam is about to return.
3. **Drag through the Firefly.** Steer the chameleon through the glow to collect it. Check the score display rather than assuming a near miss counted.
4. **Release on your chosen patch.** Let the chameleon stop, then watch the Camo meter rise. Don't keep dragging while you decide where to go next.
5. **Choose your next move only after checking the beam again.** If the next pickup needs a long crossing and you can't see a good place to stop, stay put and wait for another opening.

## Read Camo and Alert together

**Camo** shows how well the chameleon has blended with the surface underneath it. Use it to tell whether you've given the character time to settle after moving.

**Alert** shows how close you are to being caught. Watch it when a beam approaches or crosses your position. If it fills, you lose a life. With lives remaining, you restart the same round from Hide.

The meters answer different questions. Camo tells you whether your hiding setup is ready; Alert tells you whether the current exposure is dangerous. Use both before committing to another pickup.

## A simple practice drill: survive, then collect

On your first attempt, focus on surviving one round. Watch the sweep pattern without chasing a distant Firefly. On your next attempt, add one nearby pickup and stop immediately afterward. Once you can repeat that reliably, try chaining two short moves, with a pause between them.

This works better than judging every attempt by its final score. If you get caught, notice whether you moved too early, chose a stop too far away, or stayed in motion while the beam returned. Change just that decision on the next run.

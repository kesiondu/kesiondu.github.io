## What is the Parrot Dance AI video format?

The format uses an animal-dance scene for visual comedy. A lead parrot
may appear to perform exaggerated movements, while other birds or
background activity provide rhythm and context. The humor comes from
presenting animal movement as if it were a coordinated human dance.

Individual versions can differ in the number of birds, setting,
choreography, camera framing, and audio. Treat each reference clip as a distinct version rather than assuming every parrot-dance video is identical.

## What photo should you prepare?

The right input depends on whether you are generating a parrot scene
from scratch or personalizing a dance video with a pet photo.

-   Choose a sharp image with the animal's face and body visible.
-   Prefer even lighting and a simple background.
-   Include distinctive feather colors and markings.
-   Avoid images where the animal is tiny, blurred, or partly hidden.
-   Check whether the chosen tool supports parrots or other pet species.

A photo can guide the appearance of the animal, but it cannot guarantee
that all markings or anatomy remain consistent through fast movement.

## How to make a Parrot Dance AI video

### Step 1 --- Decide on the output

Choose whether you want a parrot-only scene or a personalized pet-dance
video. These are different tasks and may require different tools.

### Step 2 --- Check input requirements

Confirm whether the generator accepts one photo, multiple photos, a
reference video, or a text prompt. Do not assume the tool supports photo
replacement unless the live instructions say so.

### Step 3 --- Upload a clear image

If pet personalization is supported, use a sharp photo in which the full
animal or the required body area is visible.

### Step 4 --- Generate the video

Use the options the actual tool provides. If it offers duration, aspect
ratio, or audio settings, choose them according to your intended
platform.

### Step 5 --- Review the output

Check for species changes, distorted wings or legs, inconsistent feather
markings, flickering backgrounds, and movements that do not fit the
apparent rhythm. Watch the full clip, not just the preview image.
